CONCEPT
01 — Concept Selection

The Chosen
Concept

Idea 3 — Selected

From three concepts explored during the ideation process, Idea 3 — the Application Journey POV — was selected as the strongest direction for the Red Bull Basement campaign. It was chosen for its authenticity, relatability and its natural fit with short-form content platforms.

The Concept

A brief POV-style video in which a university student discovers Red Bull Basement, becomes excited by the opportunity, and submits an application with their own innovative idea. The video is a chronological account from discovery to action — showing how Red Bull Basement can help students bring their ideas to life.

Why It Works

Rather than a conventional advertisement, the campaign feels like a real student finding an opportunity and sharing their story. The branding manifests naturally through the student's discovery of the Red Bull Basement site, logo visibility, and the overarching theme of innovation and ambition.

HOOK
02 — First 3 Seconds

The
Hook

Opening Line
"POV: You just realised Red Bull could help launch your idea."
The Shot

The video opens with a POV handheld shot of a student scrolling through their phone or laptop, abruptly pausing on the Red Bull Basement website. The camera pans slightly as thrilling music begins.

Why It's Scroll-Stopping

The video begins with a strong POV hook and a relatable student moment. Quick cuts, campus visuals and a personal story create curiosity and emotional engagement — compelling the viewer to watch until the end.

On-Screen Text

The opening text displayed on screen reads: "POV: You have just found Red Bull Basement." — directly addressing the viewer and creating an immediate sense of personal relevance and excitement.

STORY
03 — Narrative Structure

The
Story

Beginning — Setup

Discovery

A student is sitting on campus between lectures and discovers Red Bull Basement online or via a poster. They realise it is a programme that fosters student innovation and helps bring ideas to life.

Middle — Experience

Doubt → Action

The student begins to think about their idea and starts researching and typing their application. We see quick glimpses of brainstorming, typing and visualising what their concept could become.

End — Payoff

Confidence

The student submits their application feeling motivated and excited. Final on-screen text: "Your idea could be next. Apply to Red Bull Basement."

BRAND
05 — Branding

Branded
Elements

Brand Integration

Red Bull branding is woven naturally throughout the video rather than forced into the content. The student's organic discovery of the Red Bull Basement website ensures the logo and brand identity appear in context — feeling authentic rather than promotional.

Brand Elements Used

The campaign integrates Red Bull cans and product presence, logo visibility, the application website on screen, the iconic 'Gives You Wiiings' messaging, innovation and idea-building visuals, and on-screen text referencing the Red Bull Basement initiative directly.

Why It Feels Natural

Rather than a conventional advertisement, the branding manifests through the student's natural journey — discovering the site, seeing the logo, and engaging with the application. The theme of innovation ties the Red Bull identity to the student's own ambition seamlessly.

PLATFORM
06 — Distribution & Audience

Platform &
Virality

TikTok

TikTok is where the majority of university students consume short-form video content, making it the primary platform for this campaign. The POV format is native to TikTok's content culture — meaning the video will feel organic rather than promotional, increasing the likelihood of engagement and sharing.

Instagram Reels

Instagram Reels extends the campaign's reach to a slightly broader but equally relevant demographic of university-aged students. Reels allows the same short-form content to reach audiences who may not be on TikTok, maximising visibility across two of the most widely used platforms among Gen Z.

Target Audience — Aged 18–25

If a university student sees this campaign, they will feel inspired and motivated to apply — realising that their own ideas could turn into real opportunities through Red Bull Basement.
