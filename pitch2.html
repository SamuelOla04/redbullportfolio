01 — Overview

Presenting
the Work

The pitch represented the point where all preparatory work had to be brought together and presented coherently. My contribution was both in preparation and delivery — and producing clear evidence of that process is central to this entry. As Leaper (2019) argues, the quality of a pitch lies not just in the idea but in demonstrating genuine interrogation of that idea.

The Moment
All preparatory work — research, creative direction, the script — brought together and presented. Familiar enough with the material to present with clarity rather than read from it mechanically.
Script as Backbone
Having worked through the script so thoroughly in creative direction, I knew which points carried the most weight and where the campaign idea was strongest — this shaped how the pitch was structured and paced.
Evidence of Process
The annotated working script, review notes and the framing of the concept for the audience all form part of the pitch evidence. This kind of documentation is standard practice in professional campaign work.

03 — Framework & Theory

Structuring
the Pitch

Lead with the student audience to insight creativity is underlooked in university life. Establish relevance before anything else.

I

Sustain engagement through the strong suit of the three-beat narrative arc.

Generate appetite to see the campaign action real student, real problem, real platform with genuine reach.

Action
Drive the result — students apply to Red Bull Basement. The campaign achieves its stated objective.
Audience Insight First
Mintel (2026) highlights that pitches demonstrating clear audience insight perform better than those leading with executional detail. We led with the student audience and their relationship with creativity, grounding the concept before visual and production elements were introduced.
Persuasive Structure
The AIDA model (Strong, 1925, cited in Fill, 2019) offers a framework for structuring persuasive communication. Applying it to the pitch helped prioritise what to emphasise and in what order, ensuring the presentation built logically toward a clear call to action for the student audience.
Sources: Leaper (2019), Fill (2019), Mintel (2026), Strong (1925 cited in Fill, 2019)
