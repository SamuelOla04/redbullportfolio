BRAND
01 — The Brand

Red Bull

Where it all began

Red Bulls journey all began in 1987 and is headquartered in Fuschl am See, Austria (Redbull,2025). The brand has skyrocketed into one of the most recognisable names in the world, functioning across more than 166 countries. The brand is nurtured around its iconic energy drink which contains caffeine, taurine, B-group vitamins, sucrose, glucose and water.

What makes Red Bull stand out

What makes Red Bull stand out in the market ahead of competitors isn't because of the drinks, but the brand identity it has themed around extreme sports, motorsports, and cultural events. Red Bull's marketing direction is content-led and experience-driven, positioning the brand to correspond with energy, ambition and pushing limits.

Red Bull's Competitors

Red Bull's lead in the UK market remains firm, holding its position as the leading energy drink brand alongside competitors such as Monster, Lucozade, Boost and Relentless (Mintel, 2025). This influence reflects the strength of Red Bull's brand equity and continued consumer loyalty despite an increasingly crowded market and rising competition.

BASEMENT
02 — The Initiative

Red Bull
Basement

What is Red Bull Basement?

Red Bull Basement is a global competition that invites young people to develop creative ideas that use technology to tackle real-world problems. This is designed to connect Red Bull's brand with university students — a key demographic — by giving them a platform to showcase their creativity and entrepreneurial thinking.

The Process

Selected participants are encouraged to submit their campaign ideas, with standout candidates receiving mentorship, workshops and the opportunity to pitch their ideas at a national and then global level. The competition positions Red Bull not just as an energy drink, but as a brand that actively invests in the next generation of innovators and future problem solvers.

The Aim

For this portfolio, Red Bull Basement serves as the foundation of the campaign, the aim being to raise awareness of the initiative among university students and inspire them to interact and participate. Understanding the scheme's purpose was an important first step in shaping the direction of the campaign concept.

AUDIENCE
03 — The Audience

Target
Audience

Demographic Shift

According to Mintel (2025), Gen Z accounts for 15% of the UK population. The elder half of this generation is transitioning into adulthood, entering university, and starting their first jobs. This aligns Gen Z as the ideal target audience for Red Bull, therefore making them the prime target for the Red Bull Basement campaign.

Behavioural Drivers

As young people shift into university life a phase of late nights and social events the appeal of energy drinks naturally grows. Red Bull (2025) states that caffeine helps to enhance concentration levels, whilst B-group vitamins contribute to the reduction of tiredness, both of which are directly relevant to the demands of university life.

Brand Loyalty

Targeting Gen Z students through a campaign like Red Bull Basement at this stage of their lives is not just about short-term interactions, but about connecting with a generation that will only grow more influential and financially powerful as their brand loyalties are being shaped at this very moment.
