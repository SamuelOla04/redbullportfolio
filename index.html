Hi

Hi, I'm Samuel a digital marketing student. Welcome to my portfolio for this module, which showcases my learning over the semester and my contributions during workshop sessions.

This project breaks down a campaign related to Red Bull Basement, a global initiative that encourages university students to deliver and pitch inventive ideas that use technology to solve real-world problems. Within this portfolio I outline the campaign process covering areas such as background research into the brand and its audience, the idea generation process, and the development and pitching of a final campaign concept.

Each section has been guided by both independent research and workshop participation, reflecting my growing knowledge and understanding of what it takes to plan and execute a marketing campaign from the foundation.
