IDEAS
01 — The Concepts

Three Ideas
Considered

Idea generation is an important phase in the performance of any marketing campaign. According to Marketing91 (2025), generating new ideas is vital in generating a strong backbone for any campaign or business objective. Taking time to brainstorming and thinking freely allows marketers to organise their gathering, explore multiple avenues and make more righteous decisions. In the framework of this portfolio, the idea generation process was an asset in identifying the most suitable campaign concept for Red Bull Basement cementing the final idea was not simply the first thought, but the best one.

Idea 01

UGC Micro-Influencer Unboxing

Influencers assemble a Red Bull pack and promote the Basement opportunity to their audience. Although this concept offered a wider reach, it lacked the authentic and personal storytelling needed to genuinely align with the target audience.

Idea 02

Event Attendee POV

The concept framed around A POV video following a student at a Red Bull event. Visually appealing, but felt less personal and less relevant to the Red Bull Basement application process itself.

Idea 03

Application Journey POV

Documenting a student's journey from discovering Red Bull Basement to submitting their application. Chosen for its authenticity and personalisation with short-form platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels.

PLATFORM
04 — Distribution

Platform
Strategy

TikTok

The primary platform for this campaign is to deliver short form content. The POV format is native to TikTok's content algorithm,making the video feel organic rather than promotional to the university student audience.

Instagram Reels

Extends the campaign's reach to students who may not be on TikTok, maximising reach across two of the most known platforms among Gen Z.
